Doorstep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Doorstep.

This Privacy Policy explains how Doorstep-01 ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Doorstep mobile application.

Doorstep is a real estate business management application that helps users manage property listings, clients, deals, mortgage and affordability calculations, communications, and related business activities.

2. Information We Collect

When you create and use a Doorstep account, we may collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

Account Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account password

Business and Property Information

Depending on the features you use, Doorstep may allow you to enter and manage information relating to:

  • Property listings
  • Property addresses and details
  • Buyer and renter information
  • Client information
  • Property preferences
  • Deal and pipeline information
  • Offers and transaction information
  • Mortgage and affordability information
  • Commission and fee information
  • Documents and agreements
  • Electronic signatures

You should only enter personal information about another person where you have an appropriate reason and authority to do so.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use information collected through Doorstep to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Authenticate your account
  • Provide the Doorstep service
  • Manage property listings and clients
  • Match clients with suitable properties
  • Manage deal pipelines
  • Perform mortgage and affordability calculations
  • Calculate loan-to-value ratios and related figures
  • Generate summaries and business documents
  • Facilitate electronic signatures
  • Calculate commissions, fees and rental yields
  • Facilitate communications with clients
  • Maintain and improve the application
  • Protect the security of the service
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

4. How We Share Information

We may share information where necessary to provide Doorstep's functionality, operate our services, comply with legal obligations, or protect our rights.

Depending on the features you use, information may be shared with:

  • Service providers that help us operate Doorstep
  • Communication providers when you choose to send messages
  • Electronic-signature providers where applicable
  • Hosting, database, security, analytics, or technology providers where applicable
  • Government authorities, regulators, courts, or law-enforcement bodies where legally required

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Account Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Doorstep's services, maintain your account, fulfil legitimate business requirements, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our agreements.

When information is no longer required, we will delete or anonymise it where reasonably practicable, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Doorstep account and associated personal information.

To request account deletion, contact us using the contact details below.

Where required by applicable law and Google Play policy, we will delete personal information associated with your account following a valid deletion request, subject to information that we are legally required or permitted to retain.

8. Children's Privacy

Doorstep is intended for business and professional users and is not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Third-Party Services

Doorstep may integrate with third-party services to provide certain functionality. These services may include communication, hosting, authentication, analytics, electronic signatures, payment processing, or other technology services.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies governing their processing of information.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Request restriction of certain processing
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request a copy of certain information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise applicable rights, please contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Doorstep, our services, legal requirements, or our data practices.
